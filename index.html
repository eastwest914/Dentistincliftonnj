What is Emergency Dental Care?

Emergency Dental Care refers to immediate treatment for sudden dental issues like severe tooth pain, knocked-out teeth, broken crowns, bleeding gums, or facial swelling. Its purpose is to relieve pain, stop bleeding, and prevent further damage or infection. Quick action and visiting an emergency dentist can often save your tooth and protect your overall oral health.
